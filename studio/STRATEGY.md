# Growth strategy and learnings

Read this before every production run. The weekly growth review updates it.

## Goals (Rodney, 6 Nov 2026)
- Get paid as soon as possible: join the YouTube Partner Programme.
- About 100,000 subscribers in the first year.
- Always high-quality, well-researched education, simple enough for complete beginners.
- Keep improving from data, comments and trends, with no one needing to step in.

## The monetisation rules (checked 6 Nov 2026)
- **Until 31 Jan 2027:** 1,000 subscribers AND either 4,000 public long-form watch hours in 365 days OR 10 million public Shorts views in 90 days.
- **From 1 Feb 2027 (new applicants):** 1,000 subscribers AND either 8,000 hours in 365 days OR 20 million Shorts views in 90 days. The Shorts revenue pool also needs 10 million Shorts views over a rolling 90 days.
- **Lower tier (fan funding, unchanged):** 500 subscribers AND 3,000 hours or 3 million Shorts views.
- **What this means for us:**
  - The realistic route to ad money is long-form watch hours.
  - 8,000 hours is 480,000 minutes. At about 3.5 minutes watched per view, that's roughly 140,000 long-form views in a year, or about 380 a day.
  - Shorts are mainly for discovery and subscribers.
- **Inauthentic content policy:** mass-produced or repetitive videos can be refused monetisation. Every video must be a genuinely different topic, with its own research, script, worked example and visuals. Never re-skin a template with the same narration.

## How we grow
1. **Search first.** Long videos answer questions South Africans actually type into YouTube and Google: "how to…", "… explained", "… 2026", "… South Africa". Evergreen topics keep earning watch hours for years.
2. **Timeliness second.** Ride the calendar:
   - Budget speech (Feb)
   - Tax year end and TFSA/RA deadline (end Feb)
   - Two-pot new tax year (1 Mar)
   - Tax season (Jul–Oct)
   - Black Friday (late Nov)
   - Festive spending and bonuses (Dec)
   - "January is broke" (Jan)
   - Interest-rate decisions (SARB MPC)
   - Fuel price changes (monthly)
3. **Click-through:**
   - Thumbnails show one huge rand figure on flip tiles, at most 6 words, high contrast, and the topic badge.
   - The title starts with the search phrase, then the promise.
   - Never clickbait beyond what the video delivers.
4. **Retention:**
   - The hook in the first 15 seconds states the concrete payoff (a rand figure).
   - Keep a visual change every 3–6 seconds, and include worked examples with real numbers.
   - Chapters, then a recap.
5. **Session:**
   - End screens point to the next related guide.
   - Use playlists per theme (Tax-free and retirement, Debt, Saving, Investing).
   - Shorts point to their long guide.
6. **Shorts:**
   - Post daily.
   - Each is one surprising number or rule from a long video, with the hook in 2 seconds.
   - Make the ending loop-friendly.
7. **Community:** the description invites a comment ("What should the next guide be?"). Use commenters' questions as future topics.

## Promoting Fluxr (Rodney's business)
- **When to start:** only once the channel gets views, meaning any video passes 1,000 views or the channel passes 500 subscribers.
- **Then:** add one line near the end of new descriptions. Once a video passes 10,000 views, also add a 5-second end-card mention.
- **Wording (always name the vouchers in full):**
  "Sending airtime or data to family abroad? Fluxr: buy a 1Voucher, OTT Voucher, Blu Voucher or FNB Voucher and dial *130*31026*VoucherPIN#. www.fluxr.co.za"
- **Rules:** never invent prices or offers, and never put it in the first 30 seconds.
