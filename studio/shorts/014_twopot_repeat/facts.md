## Example (illustration; calc.py re-run 18 Nov 2026)
R10,000 withdrawn in three tax years running (R30,000 out); the first would have stayed invested 20 more years, the second 19, the third 18; growth 5% a year above inflation (real, today's money), the same assumption as main02.
- 10,000 x 1.05^20 = R26,532.98; x 1.05^19 = R25,269.50; x 1.05^18 = R24,066.19
- Total R75,868.67 ("about R75,900"); growth given up R45,868.67.
- Not a prediction: returns vary; tax and fees come on top.
