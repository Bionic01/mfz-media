TITLE: 3 two-pot withdrawals = R75,900 less at retirement #TwoPot

Take R10,000 from your two-pot savings pot three years in a row and that's R30,000 out. Left in the fund for about 20 more years at 5% a year above inflation, that R30,000 would have grown to about R75,900 in today's money.

And repeat withdrawals are common: in March 2026, Momentum Corporate's FundsAtWork data showed more than 60% of claims were third withdrawals, 33% second withdrawals, and only 5% first-timers. Each withdrawal is also taxed at your marginal rate, and the fund charges a fee.

About the example: an illustration. R10,000 a year for three tax years, left invested for 20, 19 and 18 years at 5% a year above inflation (R26,533 + R25,270 + R24,066 = R75,869). Real returns vary, and tax and fees come on top.
