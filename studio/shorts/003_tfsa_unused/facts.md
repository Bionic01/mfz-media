# Short 003 facts (verified 7 Nov 2026 on the SARS page, last updated 20/03/2026)

- Annual TFSA limit R46,000 with effect from 1 March 2026 (2027 year of assessment); was R36,000. Source: SARS, Tax Free Investments.
- "Note that any portion of unused annual limit is forfeited (that is, it is not carried forward to the subsequent year of assessment)." SARS Example 1: "The unused portion ... is NOT rolled-over to the subsequent year of assessment." Source: SARS.
- Lifetime limit R500,000 per person. Source: SARS.
- 40% penalty (in the form of normal tax payable) on the excess above the annual and lifetime limits. Source: SARS.
- Annual limit counts all tax-free investments of a person together (SARS aggregation example). Source: SARS.
- The 2027 year of assessment runs 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027 (SA tax year = 1 March to end of February; 2027 is not a leap year).
- Arithmetic (python): 46,000 - 10,000 = 36,000; 500,000 / 46,000 = 10.87 -> "about 11 years at today's limit"; skipping one year -> about 12. Framed as "at today's limit" because future limits are not known.
- The Short does not claim next year's limit amount; it says only that unused space can't be caught up.
