TITLE: Skip your TFSA this year and the space is gone forever #TFSA

Every tax year you get R46,000 of tax-free savings space (from 1 March 2026). If you don't use it, it's gone. SARS says any unused part of the annual limit is forfeited and is not carried forward to the next year.

- Put in R10,000 this tax year, and the other R36,000 of space is lost on 1 March
- You can't catch up later: each year you only get that year's limit
- The R500,000 lifetime limit takes about 11 years to fill at today's limit; every year you skip adds another
- This tax year ends on 28 February 2027
- Don't go over R46,000 in total across all your TFSAs, or SARS charges a 40% penalty on the extra
