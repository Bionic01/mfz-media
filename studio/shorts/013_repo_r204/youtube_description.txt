TITLE: +0.25% repo rate = R204 more on a R1.2m bond #RepoRate

A quarter of a percent on the repo rate adds about R204 a month to a R1.2 million home loan. Prime goes from 10.75% to 11%, and the instalment from about R12,183 to R12,386: about R2,442 a year.

The trick for your own bond: every 0.25% is about R17 a month for every R100,000 you owe. Owe R800,000? About R136 a month. A cut works the same way, in reverse. The Reserve Bank's next decision is on Thursday 19 November 2026.

About the example: an illustration of a 20-year bond at prime, with equal monthly payments. Your own rate, term and bank may differ: check your statement.
