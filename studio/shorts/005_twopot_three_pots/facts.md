# Short 005 facts (verified 9 Nov 2026)

All from SARS news, "Tax directive enhancements and tax implications of the two-pot retirement system" (read 9 Nov 2026):
https://www.sars.gov.za/latest-news/tax-directives-enhancements-and-tax-implications-of-the-two-pot-retirement-system/

- The retirement fund is "split into" a Vested Component, a Savings Component and a Retirement Component (three pots).
- Vested: "the total value of the member's interest as at 31 August 2024, less 10% of that value, capped at R30 000 to be allocated to the Savings Component as seed capital." Lump-sum payment of the vested balance before retirement "will not be impacted by the two-pot retirement system" (old rules; also National Treasury two-pot FAQ, Aug 2024, per main02 facts).
- Savings: "One-third of the members retirement fund contributions, will be allocated to the Savings Component." Two-pot started 1 September 2024 (same article).
- Retirement: "Two-thirds of the members retirement fund contributions will be allocated to the Retirement Component", used to pay a pension or buy an annuity on retirement; it "cannot be taken as a lump sum" on resignation, dismissal or retrenchment and must be transferred.
- Seed: "10% of the Vested Component, capped at R30,000, whichever is lower", once-off, in addition to the one-third share.
- Savings withdrawal: "a minimum of R2000, up to the maximum value available in the member's Savings Component once every tax year."
- Example arithmetic (python): R1,500 / 3 = R500 savings; R1,500 x 2/3 = R1,000 retirement. Illustration only; actual allocation is of the member's contributions per fund rules.
- Script says "while you're still in the fund, only the savings pot can be withdrawn" (vested pot can still be cashed under old rules when leaving a pension/provident fund, so the qualifier matters).
- Not covered (could not verify today, so not stated): retrenchment-access proposals, fees, payout times.
