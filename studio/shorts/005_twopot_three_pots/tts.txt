It's called two-pot. But your retirement fund has three pots. Pot one: the vested pot. Everything you saved up to 31 August 2024. The old rules still apply to it. Pot two: the savings pot. One third of what goes into your fund from 1 September 2024. Pot three: the retirement pot. The other two thirds, locked until you retire. Your savings pot also got a once-off start: 10% of your vested pot, up to 30,000 rand. So if 1,500 rand a month goes into your fund, 500 rand goes to savings and 1,000 rand is locked away. While you're still in the fund, only the savings pot can be withdrawn: once a tax year, from 2,000 rand. The full two-pot guide is on the channel. Follow for a new money rule every day. So next time someone says two-pot, ask them: which of the three?
