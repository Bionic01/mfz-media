TITLE: Two-pot? Your retirement fund actually has 3 pots #TwoPot

It's called the two-pot system, but your retirement fund is split into three pots (SARS calls them components):

- Pot 1, vested: everything saved up to 31 August 2024. The old rules still apply to it
- Pot 2, savings: one third of contributions from 1 September 2024, plus a once-off start of 10% of your vested pot, capped at R30,000
- Pot 3, retirement: the other two thirds, locked until you retire (on resignation or retrenchment it must be transferred, not cashed)
- Example: if R1,500 a month goes into your fund, R500 goes to savings and R1,000 to retirement
- While you're still in the fund, only the savings pot can be withdrawn: once a tax year, minimum R2,000. Withdrawals are taxed at your normal rate
