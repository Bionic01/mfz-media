TITLE: R271 a month = R9,750 for a R5,000 TV #BlackFriday

"Only R271 a month" sounds cheap. On a store card at the legal maximums, a R5,000 TV paid off over 36 months costs almost R9,750: about R1,800 in interest, R2,484 in service fees and about R460 in credit life insurance. That's about R4,750 extra: almost double the price.

Example (an illustration): a R5,000 TV bought on Black Friday on a store card over 36 months at the legal maximums: interest of 21.25% a year (the repo rate of 7.25% + 14%), the R69 monthly service fee (R60 + VAT) and credit life at R4.50 per R1,000. Over 12 months the same TV costs about R6,570. Your card may charge less than the maximum: check your statement and credit agreement. Cheapest of all: save first and pay cash.
