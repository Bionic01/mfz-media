# Short 010 facts (verified 14 Nov 2026)

- Annual TFSA limit R46,000 with effect from 1 March 2026 (2027 year of assessment); lifetime limit R500,000 per person. Source: SARS, Tax Free Investments (page last updated 20/03/2026, read 14 Nov 2026).
- Penalty: "a 'penalty' (in the form of normal tax payable) of 40% on the excess amount above the annual and the lifetime limits." SARS example: R50,000 - R46,000 = R4,000 x 40% = R1,600. Source: SARS.
- Withdraw and reinvest: "That subsequent investment will be regarded as a new contribution" and "will accordingly impact on both the annual and lifetime limits"; "The same principle will apply if any portion of the capital is withdrawn and reinvested in the same tax free investment account or any other tax free investment." Source: SARS.
- Transfers: SARS page: "Transfers between tax free investments accounts are effective from 1 March 2018." National Treasury/SARS press release (R209, 2016): investors can "request a transfer ... without incurring a reduction of their annual and lifetime limits"; "Product providers will only be allowed to transfer directly to another product provider." Old Mutual transfer page (14 Nov 2026): "Your contribution is not affected as your investment is transferred from one provider to another"; must go "from company to company". Daily Maverick 14 Dec 2025: "The correct approach is an institution-to-institution tax-free transfer"; "If you simply withdraw the money and reinvest it elsewhere, the reinvestment counts as a new contribution."
- Not stated (no source found today): the transfer time limit (e.g. 10 business days). Left out of the Short.
- Lerato is an illustration: R30,000 already in a TFSA (from earlier years), R20,000 added this tax year. Arithmetic (python): 20,000 + 30,000 = 50,000; 50,000 - 46,000 = 4,000; 4,000 x 0.40 = 1,600.
