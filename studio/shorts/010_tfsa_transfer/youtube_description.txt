TITLE: Moving your TFSA? Don't withdraw it (40% penalty) #TFSA

Moving your Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) to a better provider? Don't withdraw the money and re-deposit it. SARS treats the re-deposit as a new contribution, so it counts against your annual limit (R46,000 from 1 March 2026) and your R500,000 lifetime limit. Anything over the limit carries a 40% penalty.

Example (an illustration): Lerato has R30,000 in a TFSA and has already added R20,000 this tax year. She withdraws the R30,000 and puts it in a new TFSA: that's R50,000 this tax year, R4,000 over the R46,000 limit. 40% of R4,000 is a R1,600 penalty. And withdrawals never give your lifetime limit back.

The right way: ask your NEW provider for a TFSA transfer. The money moves directly from company to company, and a proper transfer doesn't count against your limits.
