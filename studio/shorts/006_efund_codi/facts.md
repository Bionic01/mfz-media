# Short 006 facts (verified 10 Nov 2026)

All from the SARB "CODI Frequently Asked Questions" page, read 10 Nov 2026:
https://resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/Deposit-insurance/CODI-Frequently-Asked-Questions

- "CODI became operational on 1 April 2024."
- "Qualifying depositors are covered up to a maximum amount of R100 000 per depositor per bank."
- "The R100 000 coverage limit includes both the principal amount of a deposit and interest."
- Automatic: "CODI's protection is automatic, so depositors do not have to apply." Free: "Depositors will not make any payments to CODI for deposit insurance protection." (banks pay premiums)
- Covered: transactional and savings accounts; "savings accounts and term and notice accounts (demand and fixed deposits)"; "CODI will cover money market deposits if the account is held by a qualifying depositor." Cheque accounts covered (SARB proposed coverage rules 2023, item 48; transactional per FAQ).
- Not covered: "balances a qualifying depositor has in investment products", list includes unit trusts; money market unit trusts are also listed as financial institutions. SARB coverage rules: "Money market funds" not covered.
- Per bank: "CODI's protection is up to R100 000 per qualifying depositor, per bank." Mr Smith example: R80 000 + R40 000 at Bank A -> covered R100 000 of R120 000 (accounts at one bank share one limit).
- Cover applies when SARB places the bank in resolution (script says "if your bank fails").
- Not stated (not verified today): payout timing.
