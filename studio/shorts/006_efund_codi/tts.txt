If your bank fails tomorrow, how much of your savings is safe? 100,000 rand. Since 1 April 2024, bank deposits in South Africa are insured by CODI, the Corporation for Deposit Insurance. It covers up to 100,000 rand per person, per bank, and that includes interest. It's automatic and free. You don't apply, and you don't pay. Savings, cheque, notice and fixed deposit accounts are all covered. So is a money market account at a bank. But a money market unit trust is not. It's an investment, not a bank deposit. All your accounts at one bank share one limit. A second bank gets its own 100,000 rand. Where to keep your emergency fund is in the full guide on the channel. Follow for a new money rule every day.
