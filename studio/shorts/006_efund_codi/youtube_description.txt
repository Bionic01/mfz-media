TITLE: Bank fails? CODI covers up to R100,000 #EmergencyFund

If your bank fails, how much of your savings is safe? Since 1 April 2024, South African bank deposits are protected by CODI, the Corporation for Deposit Insurance:

- Up to R100,000 per person, per bank, including interest
- Automatic and free: you don't apply and you don't pay (the banks fund it)
- Covered: savings, cheque, notice and fixed deposit accounts, and money market deposit accounts at a bank
- NOT covered: investment products such as unit trusts, including money market unit trusts
- All your accounts at one bank share one R100,000 limit. Each other bank has its own limit
