# Short 004 facts (verified 8 Nov 2026)

- 2027 tax year (1 March 2026 – 28 February 2027) brackets: not exceeding R245 100 = 18%; R245 101 – R383 100 = R44 118 + 26% above R245 100; R383 101 – R530 200 = R79 998 + 31%. Primary rebate R17 820. Source: SARS, Rates of tax for individuals (page last updated 28/10/2026).
- Savings withdrawal benefit "will be taxed using the annual payment/bonus tax calculation based on the taxpayer's marginal rate of tax"; "No retirement rates, allowable deductions, exemptions or tax-free amounts will be used." Source: SARS news, Tax directive enhancements and tax implications of the two-pot retirement system (2 Sep 2024).
- "Tax will be calculated at the tax rate applicable to the individual." "Debt owed to SARS will be deducted from the withdrawal amount"; the fund deducts outstanding debt "before any payout is made to the member". A SARS debt arrangement means the withdrawal is not affected. Source: SARS media release, Tax implications of withdrawing from two-pot (23 Aug 2024).
- Minimum withdrawal R2,000, once every tax year. Source: SARS news item (2 Sep 2024).
- Under/over-deduction settled on assessment. Source: SARS media release.
- Arithmetic (python): taxable R300,000 -> 44,118 + 0.26 x 54,900 - 17,820 = R40,572. With R20,000 withdrawal (R320,000, still inside the 26% bracket, under R383,100) -> R45,772. Extra tax R5,200 (exactly 26% of R20,000). Left before fees R14,800.
- Admin fee: NOT given as a number in this Short (fee sources could not be re-checked today); the script only says the fund's fee comes off too. The long guide used ~R345 (R300 + VAT, 10X); this Short and its description state no fee amount.
- Example assumes no other income, under 65, no deductions; the fund's directive may withhold a different amount and the final tax is set on assessment.
