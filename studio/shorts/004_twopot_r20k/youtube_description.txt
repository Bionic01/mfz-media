TITLE: Two-pot: withdraw R20,000 and SARS takes R5,200 #TwoPot

Withdraw R20,000 from your two-pot savings pot and you won't get R20,000. SARS adds the withdrawal to your income for the year and taxes it at your normal (marginal) tax rate.

- Example: you earn R25,000 a month (R300,000 a year), you're under 65 and have no other income or deductions
- At 2026/27 rates that income is in the 26% bracket (R245,101 to R383,100), so the extra R20,000 is taxed at 26%
- Tax: about R5,200. Left: about R14,800, and your fund's admin fee comes off that too
- If you owe SARS, the debt is taken first, before you're paid (unless you have a debt arrangement with SARS)
- One withdrawal per tax year, minimum R2,000
- Your fund's tax directive may withhold a different amount; the final tax is worked out on assessment
