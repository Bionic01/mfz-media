TITLE: Same payment, R6 or R2? Your bank fees explained #Capitec

Same payment, same bank: one costs R6, the other R2. At Capitec in 2026 (prices from 1 March 2026):

- Immediate payment to another bank: R6
- Normal payment to another bank: R2
- The money lands in the same account; the only difference is speed

Two immediate payments a month = R144 a year. Make them normal = R48 a year. That's R96 a year saved, just by planning ahead.

Cash costs too: R10 for every R1,000 you draw at an ATM in South Africa.

Your move: open your last bank statement, find your biggest fee, and change one habit. Other banks charge differently, so check your own bank's pricing guide.
