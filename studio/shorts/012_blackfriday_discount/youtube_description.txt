TITLE: Saved R1,250, paid R320 more than full price #BlackFriday

She saved R1,250 on a TV on Black Friday: R5,000 instead of R6,250. Then she put it on a store card over 12 months. At the legal maximums, interest (about R594), the R69 monthly service fee (R828) and credit life insurance (about R151) add about R1,570. Total: about R6,570, which is about R320 more than the full price before the sale.

Example (an illustration): a R5,000 TV on a store card over 12 equal monthly payments at the legal maximums: interest of 21.25% a year (the repo rate of 7.25% + 14%), the R69 monthly service fee (R60 + VAT) and credit life at R4.50 per R1,000. Your card may charge less than the maximum: check your statement and credit agreement. The cash route: save R500 a month for 10 months and pay R5,000.
