TITLE: 25,000 clients to 26.6 million: the Capitec story #Capitec

In 2003, Capitec had about 25,000 clients. By 31 August 2026 it had 26.6 million active clients: more than a thousand times bigger in about 23 years. It started in 2001 in Stellenbosch as a small microlender, and it's now South Africa's biggest bank by number of clients.

The lesson for you: your bank cost is more than the monthly fee. Banks can also charge for debit orders, payments and cash withdrawals. Add up three months of fees on your statement, and check whether another account would cost you less.
