TITLE: R3,834 a month in a TFSA puts you over the limit #TFSA

Saving R3,834 a month in a Tax-Free Savings Account adds up to R46,008 a year. That's R8 over the R46,000 annual limit (from 1 March 2026).

- SARS charges a 40% penalty on every rand above the limit
- SARS's own example: put in R50,000, and the R4,000 extra costs you R1,600
- The limit counts ALL your tax-free accounts together
- Safe number: R3,833 a month or less (12 x R3,833 = R45,996)
