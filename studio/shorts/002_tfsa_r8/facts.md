# Short 002 facts (verified 6 Nov 2026)

- Annual TFSA limit R46,000 from 1 March 2026 (2027 year of assessment); was R36,000. Source: SARS, Tax Free Investments (page updated 20/03/2026).
- Lifetime limit R500,000 per person. Source: SARS.
- Penalty: "a 'penalty' (in the form of normal tax payable) of 40% on the excess amount above the annual and the lifetime limits." Source: SARS.
- SARS example: R50,000 invested - R46,000 = R4,000 x 40% = R1,600 penalty. Source: SARS.
- Annual limit is an aggregation across all tax-free investments per person per year (SARS example: R11,000 + R11,000 + R14,000 across three investments = R36,000). Source: SARS; BusinessTech 15 Mar 2026 ("a customer with two tax-free accounts will have an annual limit of R46,000 for both accounts").
- Arithmetic (python): 12 x 3,834 = 46,008 (R8 over); 8 x 0.40 = R3.20; 12 x 3,833 = 45,996 (under). R46,000 / 12 = R3,833.33.
- Note: the penalty test is on the annual total; no SARS "monthly limit" exists. The Short frames R3,833/month as the safe debit-order number, not as a legal monthly limit.
