TITLE: Can you cover a R10,000 emergency? Most can't #EmergencyFund

Could you pay a R10,000 emergency today, from savings? In JustMoney's 2026 "Money & Me" survey (over 5,400 responses, July–August 2026):

- Fewer than 1 in 5 respondents could cover a R10,000 emergency from savings
- Even among people earning more than R35,000 a month, 57% could not
- 43% need to borrow before month-end just to cover living costs

The fix starts small: a first goal of R2,000 in a separate savings account. R200 a month gets you there in 10 months. Then keep building towards 3 to 6 months of your essential costs (a common rule of thumb).
