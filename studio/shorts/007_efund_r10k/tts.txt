Could you pay a 10,000 rand emergency today, from savings? A JustMoney survey of over 5,400 South Africans this year found fewer than one in five could. And it's not only low earners. Even among people earning over 35,000 rand a month, 57% could not. 43% need to borrow before month-end, just to cover living costs. So when an emergency hits, it gets paid with borrowed money. The fix starts small. Aim for a first 2,000 rand, in a separate savings account. 200 rand a month gets you there in 10 months. Then keep building, towards three to six months of your essential costs.
