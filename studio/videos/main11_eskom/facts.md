## History
- Eskom was established in 1923 (Electricity Supply Commission). Coal fleet concentrated in Mpumalanga near the coalfields.
- Load shedding began in 2007 (IOL, Feb 2024: "load shedding, which began in 2007").
- 2023: close to 7,000 hours of load shedding, the worst year on record (Engineering News, 27 Nov 2023).

## Medupi and Kusile
- Each about 4,800 MW (six units of ~800 MW).
- Kusile: budget about R80bn, final cost more than R160bn, more than 11 years late; last unit (unit 6) synchronised/commercial in September 2025.
- Combined cost overruns of Medupi and Kusile more than R300bn.
  Green Building Africa, 13 Apr 2026.

## Debt relief and municipal arrears
- Eskom Debt Relief Act, 2023: up to R254bn over three years (2023/24–2025/26), with conditions.
- Municipal arrears to Eskom: R111.6bn at 31 March 2026, up 17.9% in a year ("almost eighteen percent").
  Eskom FY2026 integrated results, 31 Aug 2026 (TechCentral; SAnews).

## Eskom FY2026 (year to 31 March 2026; results 31 Aug 2026)
- Net profit R30.3bn (FY2025: R14.0bn), "more than double"; second profit in a row after eight years of losses.
- Net debt R313.3bn.
- Electricity sales 178 TWh, down 6.2% ("six percent less").
- Load shedding: 4 days, 26 hours in the year.
- Energy availability factor about 65% (from about 61%), below Eskom's 70% target.
  TechCentral, 31 Aug 2026; SAnews, 31 Aug 2026.

## Tariffs (NERSA)
- NERSA found a R54.7bn error in its own revenue decision (MYPD6); correction raised the increases.
- Eskom direct customers: +8.76% from 1 April 2026 (was 5.36%).
- Municipal bulk tariff: +9.01% from 1 July 2026 (NERSA decision 12 Mar 2026).
- 1 April 2027: +8.83% (was 6.19%): proposed, open for comment until 2 Oct 2026, "pending final approval"; NERSA has approved Eskom's 2027/28 allowable revenue of R419.4bn (EWN, 4 Sep 2026: https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/09/04/public-invited-to-comment-on-eskom-s-883-tariff-proposal). Voice: "set to rise"; screen: "proposed, pending final approval".
  EWN, 10 Feb 2026; Enerdata, 16 Mar 2026.
- "Nine hundred percent since 2007" vs inflation about 150%: electricity minister Kgosientsho Ramokgopa, 18 Aug 2026; quote used in the video: "You are not allowed to punish those who are paying, for those who are not paying."
- Revised electricity pricing policy approved by Cabinet (Aug 2026): proposes raising free basic electricity from 50 kWh a month to 200–300 kWh for qualifying households; still a proposal, implementation depends on funding and municipalities. Voice says "often fifty units a month" and "still a proposal".
