## History
- First store: Wynberg, Cape Town, 1966 (Barney Rogut and Basil Geller). TimesLIVE 17 Oct 2026.
- November 1979: Whitey Basson (with Christo Wiese's backing) bought the 8-store business from the Rogut family; R1 million for the 8 stores. Shoprite Holdings, "More about Whitey Basson": https://shopriteholdings.co.za/group/more-about-whitey-basson.html ; TimesLIVE 17 Oct 2026 ("Whitey Basson and Christo Wiese led the buyout", R1m).
- JSE listing 1986, valued at R29m (TimesLIVE 17 Oct 2026).
- Checkers bought 1991 for R55m (TimesLIVE 17 Oct 2026).
- 1994: first township store, in Soweto (TimesLIVE 17 Oct 2026).
- End of 1997: OK Bazaars bought from SAB for R1 (Shoprite Holdings, Whitey Basson page).
- Usave: started 2003 as a single small-format store; 500th store 28 Aug 2025 in Hopefield, Western Cape; 450–700 m² (eKasi stores from 5–6 shipping containers); 420+ products under R10, a R1 packet of biscuits; CEO: "Usave was designed to go where others wouldn't." Shoprite Holdings newsroom 28 Aug 2025: https://shopriteholdings.co.za/newsroom/2025/500th-usave.html
- Africa exits: Nigeria, Uganda, Madagascar (2021: NTU CAS 15 Jun 2021, https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cas/news-events/news/detail/shoprite-pulls-out-of-uganda-madagascar-and-nigeria ; IOL 24 Aug 2021); Ghana supermarkets sold effective 25 Nov 2025, Malawi effective 22 Feb 2026 (BusinessTech 1 Sep 2026). Voice says "South Africa and its neighbours" (no country count: sources differ on 7 vs 8).
- Value chain: TimesLIVE 17 Oct 2026, analyst Bhengu: Shoprite "kept control of what makes grocery profitable: its warehouses, its trucks, its stores and its brands" (paraphrased in the voice, not quoted).
- Paying suppliers later: general retail working-capital practice, said as "big retailers usually pay their suppliers later, often weeks later" (no Shoprite-specific days claimed).

## Money services
- Money Market counter-to-counter transfer: R9.99 flat per transaction, on top of the amount; up to R5,000 a day / R25,000 a month; collect at any Shoprite till with the transfer number and PIN. Checkers "Money Transfer" page (no date on page), checked 19 Nov 2026: https://www.checkers.co.za/send-money-checkers
- Money Market Account: 4 million+ customers; account-to-account transfers free and instant (app, WhatsApp, web, USSD); Shoprite Send to 70 countries; counter-to-counter at 2,800+ stores. Shoprite Holdings newsroom 18 Nov 2025: https://www.shopriteholdings.co.za/newsroom/2025/shoprite-money-market.html
- Social grants can be switched to the Money Market Account free of charge (Shoprite Holdings newsroom 14 Sep 2023): https://www.shopriteholdings.co.za/newsroom/2023/shoprite-money-market-sassa-pay-outs.html
- Not used: the R5 cash-withdrawal fee (2022–2023 sources only, not confirmed current).

## Worked example (python, 19 Nov 2026)
- 9.99 / 200 = 4.995% ("about five percent"); 9.99 / 2,000 = 0.4995% ("half a percent").
- R200 four times a month = 4 x 9.99 = R39.96 ("almost forty rand"); R800 once = R9.99 ("about ten rand"); difference R29.97 a month ("about thirty rand"), x 12 = R359.64 ("three hundred and sixty rand a year").
