## Worked example (python, 19 Nov 2026)
- Sipho: R20,000 a month = R240,000 a year: tax 18% x 240,000 - 17,820 = R25,380 (R2,115 a month; average rate 10.6%, "about eleven rand in every hundred").
- With a R20,000 13th cheque: R260,000: 44,118 + 26% x 14,900 - 17,820 = R30,172. Tax on the bonus = R4,792 (24.0%); net about R15,208. Bonus-month PAYE about R6,907 (2,115 + 4,792). First R5,100 of the bonus at 18%, the remaining R14,900 at 26%.
- Second example: R40,000 a month (R480,000) + R40,000 bonus: all in the 31% band, tax on bonus R12,400; average rate on R480,000 = 19.2%.
- RA: R5,000 contribution takes Sipho from R260,000 to R255,000 taxable (still in the 26% band): R1,300 less tax.
- Store card: R6,000 at 20% a year: about R1,200 interest a year (simple, illustration).
- Plan: R6,000 + R5,000 + R3,000 + R1,200 = R15,200 (of about R15,208).

## Method
- PAYE on a bonus: the employer works out tax on annual income with and without the bonus; the bonus is taxed at the marginal rate. Some employers spread a contractual 13th cheque over the year ("tax smoothing"); total annual tax is the same. TaxTim, "How will my 13th cheque / bonus be taxed?": https://www.taxtim.com/za/answers/13th-cheque-bonus-and-how-is-it-taxed
- 13th cheque vs performance bonus: both are remuneration subject to PAYE (TaxTim treats them the same).

## Savings and retirement (from 1 March 2026; Budget 2026, 25 Feb 2026)
- TFSA: annual limit R46,000 (was R36,000); lifetime R500,000; 40% tax on excess contributions. SARS tax-free investments: https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-free-investments/ ; TimesLIVE 25 Feb 2026: https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/2026-02-25-budget-2026-good-news-for-small-businesses-and-savers/
- Retirement fund contributions: deductible up to 27.5% of the greater of remuneration or taxable income, cap R430,000 a year (was R350,000). Same TimesLIVE; Budget Review 2026.
- Two-pot: one third of new contributions go to a savings pot that can be withdrawn once a tax year, so "most" (not all) stays locked until retirement.
