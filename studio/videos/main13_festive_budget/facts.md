## Spending and January
- Wonga Summer Spending Survey (just over 10,000 respondents), Bizcommunity 8 Dec 2025: projected festive spend "exceeding R289bn"; average planned spend R6,299 per person; food and drinks R2,261, local travel and transport R1,166, gifts R1,135; preferred gift money 49%, vouchers 13%; 22% planned to use credit providers (20% in 2024); 21% stokvel savings; 23% saved through the year; 73% not travelling.
  https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/south-africans-set-for-r289bn-festive-splurge-wonga-172496a
  Business Explainer, 7 Oct 2026, calls it Wonga's "most recent" survey (no 2026 edition yet): https://businessexplainer.co.za/economy/2026/10/07/festive-spending-nears-r300bn-as-rates-bite/
- Debt Rescue post-festive survey, The Citizen 22 Jan 2026: 58.5% "financially worse off after the festive season" ("almost six in ten"); 37% cite school fees and other back-to-school costs as their biggest expense and source of concern ("more than a third").
  https://www.citizen.co.za/business/personal-finance/almost-60-of-survey-respondents-say-they-are-worse-off-after-december
- Brand Journal, "The Festive Trading Gap" (9 Nov 2026), via The Media Online 11 Nov 2026: after price adjustment, November 2025 retail sales about 23% above the January-October monthly average; "December sales were about 45% higher"; December and January the hardest budget months (survey of 5,400+).
  https://themediaonline.co.za/2026/11/south-africa-festive-spending-report-2026/
- Absa card data (EWN 26 Feb 2026): December 2025 card spend +10% month on month, -3% year on year; credit card usage +8%. (Background only.)

## The pay gap
- Standard Bank, "The 49-day pay gap: Why December salaries fall short", 10 Dec 2024: "42 days for those usually paid on the 25th and 49 days for month-end earners".
  https://standardbank.co.za/southafrica/news-and-media/newsroom/the-49-day-pay-gap-why-december-salaries-fall-short
- Our example (python, 19 Nov 2026): paid Tue 15 Dec 2026, next pay Fri 29 Jan 2027 = 45 days ("six and a half weeks").
- 2027 school calendar: public schools open for learners on 13 January 2027, inland and coastal (BusinessTech, 23 Oct 2026): https://businesstech.co.za/news/education/881207/here-is-the-school-calendar-for-2027-and-2028/

## Credit and help
- NCR spokesperson Winnie Rabathata, EWN 15 Dec 2025: about 10 million South Africans over-indebted; "borrow wisely from registered credit providers"; "Do not let social pressures get you into debt"; save ahead of January.
  https://ewn.co.za/2025/12/15/credit-regulator-cautions-south-africans-against-january-debt-hangover
- NCR register of credit providers and debt counsellors: https://www.ncr.org.za (voice: "You can check a lender on its website").
- Unregistered lenders keeping bank cards or IDs: general warning (illegal under the National Credit Act); voice says "some will".
- Bonus tax and debt review: point to main10 (bonus) and main08 (debt review) guides.
