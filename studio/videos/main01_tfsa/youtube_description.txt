TITLE:
Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) Explained: The Complete Beginner Guide (South Africa 2026)

DESCRIPTION:
A Tax-Free Savings Account lets your money grow with no tax on interest, dividends or growth. In this guide you'll learn exactly how a TFSA works in South Africa, the two mistakes that cost people money, and how to open one, step by step.

The basics for the 2026/27 tax year:
- Yearly limit: R46,000 (from 1 March 2026), about R3,833 a month
- Lifetime limit: R500,000
- Unused yearly space doesn't carry over
- Growth doesn't count towards the limits
- Go over the limit and SARS charges a 40% penalty on the extra
- Withdrawals can't be put back without using up your limit again
