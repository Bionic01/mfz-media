## Deposit insurance (CODI)
- Operational 1 April 2024; up to R100,000 per depositor per bank, incl. interest; automatic and free; all registered banks are members. [P SARB CODI FAQ https://resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/Deposit-insurance/CODI-Frequently-Asked-Questions]
- Covers transactional, savings, notice and fixed deposits and bank money market deposits. Does NOT cover investment products (e.g. unit trusts, incl. money market unit trusts). Separate R100,000 at each bank.

## Tax on interest
- Interest exemption 2026/27: first R23,800 of SA interest tax-free under 65; R34,500 at 65+. [F TaxTim, Accounter (checked against SARS 29 Jul 2026); N Sunday Independent 4 May 2026. SARS page itself could not be fetched today.]

## TFSA (from main01, re-checked 8 Nov for Short 003)
- Annual limit R46,000 (from 1 Mar 2026), lifetime R500,000; withdrawals don't give back contribution space; 40% penalty on excess. [P SARS https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-free-investments/]

## Two-pot comparison (from main02 facts, 2026/27 tables)
- Under 65, R300,000 taxable: marginal rate 26% (R245,101–R383,100). R10,000 savings-pot withdrawal -> extra tax R2,600, plus fund admin fee (often around R300 + VAT). [P SARS rates 2026/27]

## Rules of thumb (labelled as such, not as law)
- 3–6 months of essential expenses: common guideline from financial advisers (Liberty, Lifestyle & Tech, unnamed experts); Franc Wealth Index uses ~3 months' income (Daily Maverick 22 Apr 2026).
- Starter goal R2,000 is our own suggestion (JustMoney also suggests "at least R2,000").

## Worked example (illustration; rate 6% a year compounded monthly, not a quote)
- Thandi: essentials R10,000/month -> 3 months R30,000, 6 months R60,000.
- R200/month for 10 months -> R2,045.61 (python).
- Then R1,000/month: 26 more months to pass R30,000 (R30,020.75). Total about 3 years.
- Interest on R30,000 at 6% for a year ~R1,850, far below R23,800 exemption.
