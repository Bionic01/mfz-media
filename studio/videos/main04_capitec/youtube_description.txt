TITLE:
Capitec Story: How It Became South Africa's Biggest Bank by Clients (and What Your Bank Fees Cost)

DESCRIPTION:
In 2003 Capitec had about 25,000 clients. By August 2026 it had 26.6 million, more than any other bank in South Africa. Here's the story in plain words: how a small lender from Stellenbosch took on the giants, what it did differently, and the lesson for your own pocket: how to work out what your bank really costs you each month, and how to pay less.

The story in short:
- 2000: PSG (founded by Jannie Mouton) owned a microlending business, after buying SmartFin and FinAid (68 branches)
- 1 March 2001: Capitec is founded in Stellenbosch. Michiel le Roux is the first CEO; Riaan Stassen and Gerrie Fourie are part of the founding team
- It went after lower-income workers and people who had never had a bank account, with the idea that "banking should be simple, transparent and affordable"
- Clients: about 25,000 (2003) → 24.1 million (Feb 2025) → 26.6 million (Aug 2026). Branches: 266 → 885
- Headline earnings: R30 million (2003) → R16.8 billion (year to Feb 2026). R9.5 billion in the six months to Aug 2026, up 19%
- Gerrie Fourie, the last founder running the company day to day, retired in July 2025; Graham Lee is now CEO

The lesson: your bank cost is the monthly fee PLUS a price for each thing you do (debit orders, cash withdrawals, payments). Add up 3 months of statements, find your biggest fees, and change one habit.

About the example: Lerato is an illustration using Capitec's published 2026 prices (from 1 March 2026): R7.50 monthly fee, R3 per debit order, R10 per R1,000 cash withdrawn at an ATM, R1 per payment to a Capitec client, R2 per normal payment to another bank, R6 per immediate payment, card purchases free. Her month: R7.50 + R12 (4 debit orders) + R20 (R2,000 cash) + R5 (5 same-bank payments) + R4 (2 normal payments) + R12 (2 immediate payments) = R60.50 a month, or R726 a year. Paying by card instead of drawing cash, and using normal instead of immediate payments: R32.50 a month, R390 a year, saving R336 a year. Your bank's prices will be different: check its pricing guide.

Client numbers are each bank's figure at its latest reporting period, as compiled by BusinessTech (22 Sep 2026): Capitec about 25.8 million (Feb 2026), the next biggest about 13.1 million. Capitec reported 26.6 million active clients at 31 August 2026.
