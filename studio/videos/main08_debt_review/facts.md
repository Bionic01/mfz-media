# Facts: Money From Zero main08, Debt review vs debt consolidation vs debt counselling (researched 19 Nov 2026)

Label key: [P] primary (the Act, its regulations, the regulator), [S] reputable source naming its data. Thabo is an illustration, never presented as a real person. Every figure is used only as its source states it.

## The law [P]
- National Credit Act 34 of 2005 (NCA). s79: over-indebted = unable to satisfy all obligations in a timely manner. s86(1): a consumer may apply to a debt counsellor to be declared over-indebted (debt review). Only an NCR-registered debt counsellor or a court can make the finding. The counsellor proposes a re-arrangement; a magistrate's court (or the National Consumer Tribunal by consent) makes the order. Summary verified against the Act by OurPower, "Debt review vs debt consolidation", last verified 15 Aug 2026: https://www.ourpower.co.za/tools/debt-review/debt-review-vs-debt-consolidation
- s88(1): while under review the consumer may not take new credit (except a consolidation agreement). s88(3): once a credit provider has notice, it may not enforce the debt through the courts unless the consumer defaults on the re-arranged plan. A debt-review record shows on the credit bureau file during the process (same source).
- s71: when the consumer has paid all re-arranged debts (a home loan can still be running), the debt counsellor issues a clearance certificate and the bureaux must remove the debt-review record: https://lawlibrary.org.za/akn/za/act/2005/34/eng%402021-06-30/provision/chp_4__part_B__sec_71
- "Debt counselling" and "debt review" are the same NCA process: debt counselling is the service a registered debt counsellor gives; debt review is the legal process and status (our wording; consistent with the NCR and OurPower pages).
- Debt consolidation is not a process in the Act: it is an ordinary new loan used to pay off several debts; no over-indebtedness assessment, no court, no protection; it can be secured against a home or car (OurPower, above).
- Maximum interest (NCA Regulations, reg 42, Table A): unsecured credit transactions RR + 21% a year; credit facilities (cards, store cards) RR + 14%; mortgages RR + 12%; short-term credit 5% a month on the first loan. https://www.acts.co.za/national-credit-act-2005/r489_42__maximum_prescribed_interest_and_initiation_fees.php
- Repo rate: 7.25% from 25 Sep 2026 (SARB MPC statement, Sep 2026). Next MPC 19 Nov 2026: re-check after the announcement. The voice says only "the repo rate plus twenty-one percentage points ... still well over twenty-five percent a year" (true at any repo rate above 4%). https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/statements/monetary-policy-statements/2026/september

## Fees for debt review [S: Debt Counsellors Association of SA (DCASA), on the NCR fee guideline, 2024]
- Application fee R50 (Form 16). Restructuring fee: the first month's repayment, capped at R8,000 excl. VAT (R9,000 joint). After-care: 5% of the monthly distributable amount, capped at R450 a month excl. VAT. Legal fees to the attorney are separate. https://www.dcasa.co.za/what-fees-can-a-debt-counsellor-ask-during-the-debt-review-process/
- Said in the video: "a small application fee", "the restructuring fee is capped at R8,000 before VAT", "an after-care fee of up to R450 a month, before VAT".

## How stretched people are [S]
- DebtBusters Debt Index Q2 2026 (18 Aug 2026): new debt-counselling applicants needed 64% of take-home pay to repay debt; average interest on unsecured debt 19.6% a year; 63% had one-month "payday" loans (a record). https://www.debtbusters.co.za/debt-index/q2-2026-debt-index/
- TransUnion Q2 2026 Consumer Pulse (Business Day, 16 Jul 2026): 39% of South Africans expected to miss at least one bill or loan repayment. https://www.businessday.co.za/economy/2026-07-16-39-of-south-africans-expect-to-miss-a-bill-or-loan-repayment-survey/

## Added for the side-by-side chapter (19 Nov 2026)
- Reckless lending: NCA s80-s83: a court that finds a credit agreement reckless may set aside all or part of the consumer's rights and obligations under it, or suspend its force (s83). A debt counsellor can ask for a reckless-lending investigation (DCASA lists an optional R1,500 excl. VAT fee for it). Said as "the counsellor can ask for it to be looked at as reckless lending. If a court agrees, it can change what you owe on that loan."
- Consolidation can be secured against a car or house; the lender enforces like any loan on default (OurPower, above).
