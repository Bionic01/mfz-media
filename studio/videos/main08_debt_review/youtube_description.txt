Debt review, debt counselling or debt consolidation? They sound the same, but they're not, and picking the wrong one can cost you thousands of rand or the protection you need. Here's the twist: debt counselling and debt review are the same road. Follow Thabo (an illustration), with R60,000 across a credit card, a store card and two personal loans, through both roads: a consolidation loan (why the lower monthly payment can cost about R18,800 more) and debt review under the National Credit Act (court protection, one payment a month, capped fees, no new credit until you're done). Then the one question that tells you which one fits, and three red flags.

Key figures
- New debt-counselling applicants needed 64% of take-home pay for debt repayments (DebtBusters Debt Index, Q2 2026).
- 39% of South Africans expected to miss at least one bill or loan payment (TransUnion Q2 2026 Consumer Pulse).
- Maximum interest on a personal (unsecured) loan: the repo rate + 21 percentage points a year (NCA Regulations, reg 42).
- Debt review fees (NCR fee guideline): application R50; restructuring fee = your first month's payment, up to R8,000 before VAT (R9,000 joint); after-care up to R450 a month before VAT (5% of the monthly amount); legal fees separate.

About the example: Thabo is an illustration. R60,000 at 24% a year: about R2,354 a month over 3 years (R84,743 in total) or about R1,726 a month over 5 years (R103,565 in total). Your rate and fees will differ.
