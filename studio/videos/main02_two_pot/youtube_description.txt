TITLE:
Two-Pot Retirement System Explained: What You REALLY Get When You Withdraw (South Africa 2026)

DESCRIPTION:
Thinking of a two-pot withdrawal? Watch this first. In this guide you'll learn how the three pots work, what a savings pot withdrawal really costs you after tax and fees, the 4 traps that catch people, and how to withdraw the right way, step by step.

The basics (2026/27 tax year):
- The two-pot system started on 1 September 2024
- Vested pot: everything saved up to 31 August 2024 (old rules)
- Savings pot: 1/3 of contributions, can be withdrawn once per tax year, minimum R2,000
- Retirement pot: 2/3 of contributions, locked until you retire
- Withdrawals are taxed at your normal tax rate, plus an admin fee, and SARS takes any tax debt first

About the example: someone under 65 earning R25,000 a month (R300,000 a year) withdraws R20,000. At 2026/27 tax rates the extra tax is about R5,200 (26%), plus a fee of about R345, leaving about R14,450. Your fund's tax directive may withhold a different amount; the final tax is worked out on assessment. The growth figure (about R86,000 after 30 years) is an illustration only, not a promise: 5% a year above inflation, in today's money.
