# Two-pot fact sheet (verified 5 Nov 2026 by research agent)
P = primary (SARS/Treasury/gov.za/GEPF), F = fund/industry, N = news

## Start & purpose
- Started 1 Sept 2024; balances valued 31 Aug 2024. [P SARS https://www.sars.gov.za/latest-news/tax-directives-enhancements-and-tax-implications-of-the-two-pot-retirement-system/]
- Aim: improve retirement outcomes (preservation) + emergency access without resigning. Revenue Laws Amendment Act 2024; Pension Funds Amendment Act 31 of 2024. [P gov.za]

## Three pots
- Vested pot: everything up to 31 Aug 2024 minus seed. No new contributions; keeps growing. Old rules apply. [P Treasury FAQ https://archive.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2024/2024%20Two-pot%20System%20Updated%20%20FAQ%20August%202024.pdf]
- Savings pot: 1/3 of contributions from 1 Sept 2024 + seed. Accessible pre-retirement.
- Retirement pot: 2/3 of contributions; locked till retirement.
- Seed: once-off 10% of vested value at 31 Aug 2024, max R30,000. Not annual.
- GEPF (defined benefit): 4 months of service per year to savings, 8 to retirement; same seed.

## Savings-pot withdrawals
- Min R2,000; one withdrawal per tax year (1 Mar–end Feb); no max beyond balance. [P Treasury FAQ]
- (Unclear if once-a-year is per fund or across funds -> "check with your fund")
- Tax: added to taxable income, taxed at marginal rate; retirement tax-free amounts don't apply; must be tax registered. [P SARS]
- Fund withholds per SARS directive; final tax on assessment, can be more. [F]
- SARS debt taken first (IT88L); formal payment arrangement stops it. [P https://www.sars.gov.za/two-pot-retirement-system/]
- Directive rejected if outstanding returns / details mismatch; can't cancel once sent to SARS. [P]
- Fees: flat admin fee; 2024 survey average ~R320; 10X R300 excl VAT; no cap. [F moonstone, 10x]
- Payout: Sanlam ~10 working days (Nov 2025); GEPF brochure up to 60 working days. [F]

## Retirement pot
- No cash before retirement, not on resignation/retrenchment; stays or transfers. [P SARS]
- At retirement buys an annuity (living or life). [F Allan Gray]
- De minimis from 1 Mar 2026: overall R360,000 (was R247,500); full cash if 2/3 of non-vested vested + full retirement pot <= R240,000 (was R165,000). [P Budget Review 2026 Table 4.6] (omitted from Rates Act; SARS applies anyway)
- Exceptions: death -> beneficiaries; emigration -> after 3 unbroken years tax non-residency. Ill health = early retirement per fund rules (unconfirmed).
- Before Sept 2024 you could cash whole fund on resignation; now only vested + savings.

## Vested pot
- Old rules: pension/provident can cash on resignation (taxed) or transfer. [P]
- Lump-sum tables 2026/27 unchanged: withdrawal first R27,500 tax-free; retirement/retrenchment first R550,000 tax-free. [P https://www.sars.gov.za/tax-rates/income-tax/retirement-lump-sum-benefits/]
- RAs: no access before 55 except disability, <R15,000, emigration. Unemployment doesn't count (PFA rulings). [N]
- At retirement usually up to 1/3 cash, 2/3 annuity; provident 55+ on 1 Mar 2021 may take 100%. Preservation funds: one pre-retirement withdrawal (vested). [F]

## 2025–2026 changes
- LAW: Revenue Laws Amendment Act 2025 (Act 6 of 2025, 24 Dec 2025): court-ordered deductions proportional from all pots; provident 55+ on 1 Mar 2021 stay excluded; funds choose seed valuation date. [N/F]
- Provident 55+ on 1 Mar 2021: excluded unless opted in; window closed 31 Aug 2025; irreversible.
- LAW: Taxation Laws Amendment Act (gazetted 1 Apr 2026): on leaving fund you can take full savings-pot balance even if already withdrew that tax year (from 1 Mar 2026 per one fund). (Act not read directly)
- Budget 2026 (25 Feb 2026): de minimis change; retirement deduction cap R350k -> R430k; no change to R2,000 min, 1/3-2/3 split, or tax on savings withdrawals. [P Budget Review https://www.treasury.gov.za/documents/National%20Budget/2026/review/FullBR.pdf]
- PROPOSAL ONLY: retrenchment access to retirement pot (severe distress; savings pot + UIF exhausted; instalments). Treasury talks later 2026; Cosatu wants 2027; ASISA opposes. [N BusinessDay 16 Mar 2026]
- GEPF: new actuarial factors 1 Oct 2025 cut pre-retirement exit benefits ~15% avg. [N]

## Usage stats
- To 31 Jan 2025: 2,664,279 applications; 2,403,379 approved; R43.42bn gross. [P SARS media release]
- 1 Sep 2024 – 28 Feb 2026 (SARS figures): R79.3bn approved, R21.4bn tax, 5.6m directive applications. [N BusinessDay] <- headline figure
- GEPF 2024/25: 691,501 applications, 564,547 paid, R14.37bn, avg R23,554. [P GEPF]
- March 2026: Alexforbes 140,000+ claims in first week; Momentum 62% of claims were third withdrawals; avg claim R12,666 (Sept 2024) -> R9,290 (Mar 2026). [N Daily Maverick 22 Mar 2026]
- SARS flagged 213,654 taxpayers who understated income. [P SARS Jan 2025]

## Worked example (2026/27 tables)
- Tables: 18% to R245,100; then R44,118 + 26% above R245,100 (to R383,100); primary rebate R17,820.
- Under 65, taxable income R300,000 (R25,000 x 12). R20,000 withdrawal. Fee R345 (R300 + VAT).
- Tax without: R40,572. With: R45,772. Extra tax R5,200 (26%).
- In hand ~R14,450–R14,550, less any SARS debt.
- Growth given up (5% real, today's rands): R32,578 (10y), R53,066 (20y), R86,439 (30y).

## Mistakes / warnings
- Lost growth; Treasury urges advice. Tax surprise; directive may under-withhold. SARS debt first; returns block; irreversible.
- Flat fee: R320 = 1.6% of R20,000 but 16% of R2,000.
- Repeat/non-emergency withdrawals (Sanlam: some use for online gambling).
- Myth: can access vested/retirement pots, or withdraw several times a year.
- Scams: only official channels; never share OTPs/passwords with "helpers" (administrator warnings).

## How to withdraw
1. Check: >= R2,000 in savings pot, no withdrawal yet this tax year.
2. Tax registered, returns filed, name/ID/bank details match.
3. Apply via fund portal/app or employer HR.
4. Fund verifies and asks SARS for tax directive (IRP3(a)); only funds/employers/insurers can request.
5. SARS sets tax + debt; fund pays net into verified bank account (days to ~2 weeks; GEPF up to 60 working days).
6. Don't apply in late February (may count against next tax year, GEPF).
